Source. Ohio Medicaid Provider Remittance Advice (PNM), service code T1019 personal care. One remittance is one weekly payment.

Dates. The paid date is the remittance date; the filed date is taken from the claim control number; service periods are the visit dates on each claim.

Figures. Billed and paid are the adjudicated amounts. A denied claim pays nothing until corrected and resubmitted. Hours are verified fifteen-minute units.

Privacy. Recipients are shown as masked labels; no names or Medicaid identifiers appear on this statement. Identified detail is held in Peniel's records.
